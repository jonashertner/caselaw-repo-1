OpenCaseLaw — bulk access notice
================================

You have been rate-limited after a large number of individual export
requests. If you only wanted one or two files, this should not have
happened — please write to us and we will fix it.

This is not a refusal. The corpus is CC0 — public domain, no conditions,
no attribution required — and you are welcome to all of it. We would just
rather you took it in one piece than a million at a time:

    https://huggingface.co/datasets/voilaj/swiss-caselaw

That mirror carries the full corpus (1,000,000+ Swiss court decisions,
federal and cantonal statutes) as Parquet, rebuilt daily. For citation
metadata in particular it is strictly better than scraping per-record
exports: consistent fields, no HTML, no rate limit, one download.


Commercial and high-volume users
--------------------------------
If you are building a product on this data, or need volume beyond what
the mirror gives you, please get in touch and we will agree an
arrangement that works for you — higher or unmetered limits, a different
format, a live feed, or a direct data transfer:

    team@jonashertner.com

We would genuinely rather negotiate something that fits than throttle
you. Identified users get better access than anonymous ones, and the
conversation costs you one email.


Why the limit exists
--------------------
Per-record export reads compete for database page cache with our nightly
corpus rebuild. At sustained volume they degrade the service for
interactive users and have, at least once, disrupted our own publication
pipeline. The mirror exists precisely so that bulk consumers never need
to do this.


Other access paths, all free and unauthenticated:
  - REST API + MCP server:  https://mcp.opencaselaw.ch
  - Documentation:          https://opencaselaw.ch
  - Source code (MIT):      https://github.com/jonashertner/opencaselaw
