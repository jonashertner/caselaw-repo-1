OpenCaseLaw — bulk access notice
================================

You are requesting individual export files at high volume and have been
rate-limited.

This is not a refusal. The corpus is CC0 — public domain, no conditions —
and you are welcome to all of it. We would just rather you took it in one
piece than a million at a time:

    https://huggingface.co/datasets/voilaj/swiss-caselaw

That mirror carries the full corpus (1,000,000+ Swiss court decisions,
federal and cantonal statutes) as Parquet, rebuilt daily. For citation
metadata in particular it is strictly better than scraping per-record
exports: consistent fields, no HTML, no rate limit, one download.

Why the limit exists: per-record export reads compete for database page
cache with the nightly corpus rebuild. At sustained volume they degrade
the service for interactive users and have, at least once, disrupted our
own publication pipeline.

If the mirror does not fit what you are building — you need a format we
do not publish, a live feed, or a higher interactive rate — please write
to us. We are a public research project and we would rather help you than
throttle you, and identified users can be granted higher limits:

    team@jonashertner.com

Other access paths, all free and unauthenticated:
  - REST API + MCP server:  https://mcp.opencaselaw.ch
  - Documentation:          https://opencaselaw.ch
  - Source code (MIT):      https://github.com/jonashertner/opencaselaw

— OpenCaseLaw
