OpenCaseLaw.ch

Two months ago there was no open, machine-readable archive of Swiss case law. Today there is.

965,000 court decisions from every federal court and all 26 cantons. 5,500 federal laws and 26,000 cantonal legislative texts in German, French, and Italian. 8.8 million citation links between decisions. The legislative history — Federal Council Botschaften — for 2,500 laws. Scholarly commentary from OnlineKommentar.ch. Updated daily. CC0 data, MIT code.

On average, more than 800 users per day (est.) — lawyers, judges, researchers, law students, developers — across Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini, Grok, a Microsoft Word add-in, and direct API integrations. Traffic patterns suggest the data is also being quietly picked up by larger platforms.

What it does, concretely:

Search case law in natural language across the full corpus, in any of the three official languages. Get back ranked results with court name, citation count, cited statutes, and a leading-case flag.

Pull a structured case brief for any decision: Key holdings, Facts, key court considerations with sub-paragraph references, every statute the court relied on, incoming and outgoing citation counts.

Trace who cites a landmark ruling. Map the full citation network around any decision — incoming, outgoing, by confidence score. Check whether a precedent still holds or has been narrowed.

Find the leading cases for any statute article, ranked by citation authority. Get the doctrine overview: statute text, authority-ranked BGE list, doctrinal timeline across decades, scholarly commentary.

Look up any federal or cantonal statute article and get the current text alongside the Federal Council Botschaft that proposed it — legislative intent, key arguments, design choices. For the Bundesverfassung: the full structured Botschaft digest and the original parliamentary debate transcripts from both chambers.

Search in plain language. The platform translates everyday terms to legal vocabulary: "Vaterschaftsurlaub" finds Art. 329g OR even though the statute says "Urlaub des andern Elternteils." Search for dog-leash rules in Geneva and get the right article back in French, from a German query.

Generate law exam questions from real Bundesgericht fact patterns — the model analysis stays hidden until the student submits. Draft a mock decision grounded in real case law and statutes.

Insert correctly formatted Swiss legal citations into Word without leaving the document using the Word Add-in. Verify a quoted passage in a brief against the actual decision text (with Pro features).

23 tools at mcp.opencaselaw.ch — no API key, no sign-up. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini, Windsurf, and any MCP client. For Grok and other function-calling LLMs, OpenAI-compatible tool definitions are published at the repo.

No cookies. No user accounts. No query logging.
