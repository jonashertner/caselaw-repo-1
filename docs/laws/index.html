Swiss federal and cantonal laws

Full text of 5,510 federal laws (mirrored from Fedlex SPARQL) plus 15,722 cantonal acts (LexWork direct + LexFind fallback). Article-level search with snippets. Look up any article by abbreviation + number. Free, no account, no API key.

    Look up an article directly

    Enter the law's standard abbreviation (OR, ZGB, STGB, STPO, ZPO, BV, …) and an article number. Returns the verbatim text from the local Fedlex mirror in <1 ms.
